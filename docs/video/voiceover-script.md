## Check before recording
- **UPI, October:** confirm 25.5 billion transactions and the dollar value on NPCI's monthly statistics page.
- **Remittances:** $685 billion to developing countries is the World Bank's 2024 figure. For India, the World Bank's 2024 number is about $129 billion. RBI's FY2024-25 figure is about $135 billion. Use whichever source you can cite.
- **Fees:** the World Bank's global average cost of sending money is a little over 6%, which is why the script says "around six percent".
- **40 million:** the pitch deck counts 40 million users across W Group, not WhiteBIT customers who hold USDT. The script follows the deck.
- **World ID:** the World App approval in the video is simulated (the card says so). The script describes the real product flow.

## Recording tips
- Play the video muted and read along. If you stumble, record block by block and line each one up to its start time.
- Leave about half a second of silence at each cut. The cuts already cross-fade.
