# Build the missing switch, not another chain

As of 9 November 2026, nobody has built "UPI for stablecoins", and the missing piece is not a faster chain. Settlement already works: Arc, Tempo, Stable and Monad all finalize in about a second or less, Tron moves about **$24B of USDT a day** ([Cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/tron-leads-usdt-stablecoin-transfer-volume/)), and gas can already be hidden from users at the protocol level on Celo, Tempo and Stable ([Celo fee currency](https://docs.celo.org/developer/fee-currency); [Tempo fee sponsorship](https://tempo.xyz/developers/docs/accounts/fees/fee-sponsorship.md); [Stable docs](https://docs.stable.xyz/llms.txt)). What is missing is the layer UPI gets from NPCI: one shared name directory, one request and QR format every wallet accepts, mandatory refund and turnaround rules, and safe mandates. UPI is described as a neutral switch where "the app does not hold funds, and the bank does not own the user" ([NPCI UPI product overview](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview)). Crypto has a live equivalent for most UPI layers, from ENS names to CCTP burn-and-mint ([ENSIP-11](https://docs.ens.domains/ensip/11); [Circle CCTP](https://developers.circle.com/cctp/cctp-supported-blockchains)), but each one is locked to a chain or a wallet, and the research found no consortium writing a shared rulebook. The gaps persist for three reasons. Interop standards such as ERC-7930 and ERC-7828 are still in Review ([EIP-7930](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7930)). Anyone who takes funds in on one chain and pays out on another looks like a money transmitter or CASP to regulators ([FinCEN 2019 guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf); [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-04/ESMA75-113276571-1679_Statement_on_the_end_of_transitional_periods_under_MiCA.pdf)). And low-fee stablecoin rails have no interchange to pay for disputes ([Mastercard chargeback cost](https://b2b.mastercard.com/news-and-insights/blog/what-s-the-true-cost-of-a-chargeback-in-2025)). That points WondrPay v2 at a specific opening: a non-custodial handle resolver, signed request/QR format, receipt and refund ledger, and mandate layer that sits on top of issuer-native rails (CCTP, Gateway, USDT0). It should use Whitechain as its distribution corridor and avoid becoming a bridge, a router or a custodian.

## UPI works as five shared layers, and crypto owns only fragments of each

UPI's architecture comes down to a rule about who owns what. NPCI runs the switch between payer PSP, payee PSP and banks, and the banks settle net positions through RBI accounts in several cycles a day ([NPCI](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview)). Third-party apps such as PhonePe and Google Pay connect through sponsor PSP banks and **never hold customer funds** ([NPCI TPAP list](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/3rd-party-apps)). A UPI ID is an alias. NPCI's mapper lets a mobile number resolve to a VPA from any app ([NPCI UPI Numeric ID](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/upi-numeric-id)). Authentication is device binding plus a PIN typed into an NPCI-supplied library that the app never sees ([NPCI](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview)). The scale this produces is enormous: **25.47B transactions in October 2026**, about 822M a day ([Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/upi-hits-record-25-47-bn-transactions-in-october-daily-value-crosses-inr-1-l-cr)).

UPI's history carries three lessons a stablecoin clone should take to heart. First, pull payments are dangerous. NPCI stopped processing P2P collect requests from 1 October 2025 because of fraud, and merchant collect continues ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/npci-to-end-upi-p2p-collect-requests-from-october-1-to-reduce-fraud)). Second, zero fees do not pay for themselves. The FY27 subsidy is **₹2,000 crore** ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/budget/news/upi-rupay-subsidy-fy27-rs-2000-crore-mdr-126020100741_1.html)) against an estimated **₹20,700 crore** annual operating cost ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/20700-crore-upi-problem-why-govt-is-considering-merchant-charges-again)). A 0.4% MDR on merchant payments above ₹2,000 was approved and then deferred past Diwali 2026 ([Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/upi-hits-record-25-47-bn-transactions-in-october-daily-value-crosses-inr-1-l-cr)). Third, free rails concentrate the market. PhonePe (45.57%) and Google Pay (32.23%) still exceed the 30% per-app cap that comes into force on 31 December 2026 ([ProductGrowth](https://productgrowth.in/insights/fintech/phonepe-google-pay-upi-share-september-2026/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/tech-today/news/story/npci-extends-30-upi-market-share-cap-deadline-on-3rd-party-apps-to-december-2026-459171-2024-12-31)). UPI also has features crypto lacks entirely. **UPI Circle** lets a primary user delegate spending to a dependant with caps of ₹5,000 per transaction and ₹15,000 per month ([Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/upi-circle-launched-for-designated-secondary-users-know-features-monthly-cap-guidelines-for-usage-rbi-shaktikanta-das-443493-2024-08-29)). RBI's turnaround-time rules require failed transactions to be auto-reversed within T+1, with compensation for delays ([RBI TAT circular](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693)).

### What the newest payment chains do that general-purpose chains cannot

**Tempo** puts the most payment machinery into the protocol itself. A transaction qualifies for the payment lane only if every call is an allow-listed TIP-20 payment, and a header field caps gas for everything else, so non-payment traffic cannot crowd out payments ([Tempo payment lane spec](https://tempo.xyz/developers/docs/protocol/blockspace/payment-lane-specification.md)). Its 32-byte memos and virtual addresses give merchants invoice reconciliation without sweep transactions ([Tempo virtual addresses](https://tempo.xyz/developers/docs/protocol/tip20/virtual-addresses.md)). Under its TIP-403 receive policies, a blocked payment can be held for a recovery authority instead of reverting ([Tempo receive policies](https://tempo.xyz/developers/docs/protocol/tip403/receive-policies.md)). That is the nearest thing to a protocol-level "failed payment, auto-reversal" primitive in the notes. Tempo's validators include Visa, Stripe and Zodia Custody ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-04-14-visa-stripe-zodia-custody-standard-chartered-validators-tempo-397286)). It raised **$500M at a $5B valuation** ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/375152/stripe-tempo-500-million-series-a-thrive)).

**Arc** is the issuer's own chain. USDC is the gas, the balance and the compliance perimeter. Transfers to or from blocklisted addresses revert at the protocol level and still burn gas ([Arc EVM differences](https://docs.arc.io/arc/references/evm-differences.md)). Developers can call App Kit swaps between USDC and EURC and charge their own spread fee per swap without writing contracts ([Arc stablecoin FX](https://docs.arc.io/build/stablecoin-fx.md)). Its founding validators include BlackRock, DTCC, Visa and Mastercard ([Circle Q2](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-reports-second-quarter-2026-results)). Two cautions apply. Native paymaster sponsorship is still "planned, not yet available" ([Arc docs](https://docs.arc.io/arc/concepts/system-overview.md)). And no source in the notes confirms that Circle Payments Network actually settles on Arc today, even though CPN reached **$41.3B annualized TPV** in Q3 2026 ([Circle Q3](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-reports-third-quarter-2026-results)).

**Stable** and **Plasma** both make USDT gasless for the user, in different ways. Stable makes USDT0 the gas asset and offers governance-registered waivers that run user payloads at zero gas price ([Stable docs](https://docs.stable.xyz/llms.txt)). Plasma keeps XPL as gas but sponsors plain `transfer()` calls through a protocol paymaster, which comes with per-account rate limits ([Equilibrium](https://equilibriumlabs.substack.com/p/equilibrium-infra-bulletin-52-the)). On-chain float tells a different story from the marketing. DefiLlama shows **Plasma at $1.42B, Tempo at $812M and Stable at just $30M** ([DefiLlama](https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoinchains)). Plasma's XPL token is down about 95% from its peak ([Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-plasma-chain)).

**Monad** is a fast general-purpose EVM chain, not a stablecoin chain. It is a strong place to deploy unmodified Solidity contracts, but it has no protocol-level stablecoin gas, so sponsorship is the app's job ([Monad docs](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/gas-pricing)). It also has two quirks that can trap a payments team. Gas is billed on the gas limit, not gas used, and an EOA delegated through EIP-7702 cannot fall below a **10 MON** balance ([Monad differences](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/differences)). Real load was about 3.5M transactions a day in July 2026 ([Monad blog](https://www.monad.xyz/blog/monad-economy-eight-months-in)), roughly 40 TPS against a 10,000+ TPS design capacity. Monad's payments strategy runs through partners: MetaMask has sent more than 1M gasless transactions on it ([Monad blog](https://monad.xyz/blog/monad-ecosystem-highlights-august-2026)), and Rain cards, zerohash and Abound are live there ([Monad blog](https://monad.xyz/blog/monad-ecosystem-highlights-august-2026)).

### Whitechain gives WondrPay distribution, not payments primitives

Whitechain became an OP Stack L2 on 26 October 2026. It kept chain ID 1875, and testnet "Whitechain Sepolia" is chain 1874 ([migration docs](https://docs.whitechain.io/learn/general/migration.md)). For payments it is a general-purpose L2: gas is paid only in WBT, and no gasless or paymaster feature is documented ([network fees](https://docs.whitechain.io/learn/network/network-fees.md)). Soft confirmations come from one designated sequencer that can reorder or drop them before the batch lands on Ethereum ([finality docs](https://docs.whitechain.io/learn/network/transaction-finality.md)). Stablecoin liquidity is thin today. **Official USDT on mainnet arrives "in later releases, each after its audit"**, and the Portal bridge that carries Tron USDT is centralized: it has a backend signing key and an admin pause ([Portal docs](https://docs.whitechain.io/build/bridge/portal-bridge)). Its real asset is reach. W Group claims 40M+ users and WhiteBIT 10M+ ([Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/whitechain-obyavila-o-perezapuske-kak-l2-set-na-baze-op-stack/)), WB Soul gives KYC'd users an on-chain identity with no personal data on chain ([WhiteBIT help](https://help.whitebit.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11905736649885)), and the Builders Program pays up to **$300k per project** in milestone tranches ([Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/whitechain-obyavila-o-perezapuske-kak-l2-set-na-baze-op-stack/)).

These facts change how the existing build should be read. WondrPay's gasless USDT relayer fills a gap the chain itself does not fill. The testnet USDT at `0xaa79…8f46` is the Portal-bridged token, and mainnet will need a different, post-audit address ([Portal docs](https://docs.whitechain.io/build/bridge/portal-bridge)). Three technical traps follow from the migration. `eth_chainId` returns the same value on the legacy L1 and the L2 ([migration docs](https://docs.whitechain.io/learn/general/migration.md)). Mainnet enforces a 16,777,216 per-transaction gas cap that Sepolia does not, so a transaction that passes on testnet can fail on mainnet ([network reference](https://docs.whitechain.io/learn/network/reference)). And W Group already runs a separate custodial processor called **Whitepay**, charging 0.4–1.5% pay-in fees ([whitepay.com](https://whitepay.com/)), which vindicates the rename to WondrPay. Whitepay's own pages do not mention Whitechain ([whitepay.com](https://whitepay.com/)). W Group's payment products settle on the exchange ledger, which leaves a self-custodial, on-chain payments layer on Whitechain unclaimed.

Three patterns from this table should shape WondrPay's design. First, **every free transfer is an internal transfer**. Binance Pay is free only Binance-to-Binance ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/stable-launches-stablepay-to-bring-stablecoin-payments-to-the-mainstream/)), World Money usernames reach only other World Money users ([BitcoinEthereumNews](https://bitcoinethereumnews.com/tech/world-rolls-out-money-app-as-key-features-remain-country-limited/)), and Venmo's external crypto sends are irreversible ([Venmo](https://help.venmo.com/hc/en-us/articles/16145303480595-Crypto-Transfers)). Second, **cards, not crypto QR, are how self-custodial apps reach shops**. MiniPay, Plasma One, KAST, RedotPay and ether.fi all lead with a Visa card, and stablecoin card spending passed **$1B a month** ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/08/23/crypto-card-spending-tops-usd1-billion-as-stablecoins-move-into-everyday-purchases)). ### Merchant gateways settled on 1–1.5% with fiat payout and no disputes

On the merchant side, the incumbent model is "accept stablecoin, settle fiat". Stripe accepts USDC on Tempo, Ethereum, Solana, Polygon and Base and settles to the merchant's local currency ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/stablecoin-payments)). It charges a reported **1.5%** that covers conversion, AML screening and gas ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15083174-stripe-stablecoin-payments-2026)). Its docs say **"Dispute support: No"**, and refunds always go back as stablecoin to the original wallet ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/stablecoin-payments?locale=en-GB)). Stripe acceptance is generally available only to US businesses, with the EU, Hong Kong and Mexico in private preview ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/stablecoin-payments)). PayPal's Pay with Crypto is US-only and excludes New York ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/07/28/paypal-expands-crypto-payments-us-merchants-lower-cross-border-fees/)). Crypto-native gateways quote 0.5–2% headline fees but add payout and conversion charges. BitPay is 2% + $0.25 for small merchants ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14895614-stablecoin-payment-processor-fees-compared-2026)). The card networks use stablecoins in the back office. Visa runs about a $7B annualized settlement rate across nine chains ([Transak](https://transak.com/blog/visa-mastercard-stablecoin-settlement)), and Mastercard closed its BVNK acquisition for up to $1.8B on 3 August 2026 ([Mastercard IR](https://investor.mastercard.com/investor-news/investor-news-details/2026/Mastercard-completes-acquisition-of-BVNK-to-advance-global-stablecoin-capabilities/default.aspx)). No merchant-grade stablecoin product was found for India, and the best-known Indian ramp is named in an Enforcement Directorate probe ([XFlowPay](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/best-stablecoin-off-ramp-platforms-india); [Cryptonewsnavigator](https://www.cryptonewsnavigator.com/news/cryptopolitan/indias-ed-targets-on-rampoff-ramp-firms-in-cross-border-crypto-probe-64290264)).

### Cross-chain movement is solved for USDC, partly solved for USDT, and risky everywhere else

Issuers' own rails now cover most stablecoin movement. **CCTP V2** burns and mints native USDC across about 31 domains, including Arc, Monad, Plasma, Solana, Base and World Chain. Fast Transfer settles in about **8–20 seconds** ([Circle CCTP](https://developers.circle.com/cctp/cctp-supported-blockchains); [Circle Gateway docs](https://developers.circle.com/gateway)) for about **1 bps** on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base and Solana, and Standard Transfer is free ([Circle fees](https://developers.circle.com/cctp/concepts/fees)). **Circle Gateway** keeps a unified, non-custodial USDC balance that mints on another chain in under 500 ms and offers a 7-day trustless exit ([Circle Gateway](https://developers.circle.com/gateway)). **USDT0** uses LayerZero OFT burn and mint on about 24–25 networks ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15291261-best-cross-chain-usdt-bridge-2026-multi-network-steps)). Tron cannot host native OFT, so it connects only through a liquidity-based "Legacy Mesh" ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15291261-best-cross-chain-usdt-bridge-2026-multi-network-steps)). Everything else runs through intent bridges or abstraction vendors, and those carry vendor risk. Across lost under $4M of relayer capital in July 2026 to forged off-chain events ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/07/24/across-solana-relayer-attack/)), and OneBalance deprecated its chain-abstraction API in May 2026 ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/migrating-from-onebalance-to-zerion-api/)).

### Agentic payments are real infrastructure with inflated volume

The agent stack now has distinct layers. Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol and Mastercard's Agentic Tokens handle agent identity. Google's AP2 handles the user mandate. Stripe and OpenAI's ACP handles checkout. **x402** handles stablecoin settlement, and the Linux Foundation has governed it since 2 April 2026 ([Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-is-launching-the-x402-foundation-and-welcoming-the-contribution-of-the-x402-protocol)). Mastercard Agent Pay blocks a purchase above the user's set limit at the network unless the user gives fresh authorization ([ATXP](https://atxp.ai/blog/mastercard-agent-pay-stripe-acp-visa-x402-comparison-2026/)). Usage claims fall apart under scrutiny. TRM Labs analysed about **198.9M x402 settlements worth about $52.7M** and estimates that only **0.6–7.5% of the value was actually agentic** ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/agentic-payments-are-growing-most-x402-payments-are-not-from-ai-agents)). In October 2026, a disclosed x402 SDK flaw let malicious APIs trick agents without spend limits into authorizing their entire balance ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-x402-sdk-flaw-could-let.html)). x402 has no built-in dispute or refund path ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/genlayer-x402-ai-agent-payment-disputes/)). On the consumer side, the clearest large deployment is Revolut's AIR assistant. It reached about 13M UK customers and requires biometric approval for any sensitive action ([FinTech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/news/revolut-air-ai-assistant-uk-customers-launch-2026); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/revolut-launches-ai-assistant-air-in-ireland-and-europe)).

Each gap below has a technical cause, a compliance cause and an economic cause, and in most cases the technical part is the easiest to fix.

### No universal address or QR, because "any chain" means becoming a bridge and a money transmitter

The technical barrier is real but shrinking. Ethereum's ERC-55 address format is reused by many chains, yet it "doesn't say which chain an address is meant for" ([EIP-7930](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7930)). ERC-7828's `alice.eth@base` syntax exists to stop funds going "to an unreachable address on the wrong chain" ([EIP-7828](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7828)). Both standards were still in **Review** on 9 November 2026 ([EIP-7930](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7930); [EIP-7828](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7828)). Contract wallets add a sharper danger. In 2022 an attacker replayed a Safe deployment on Optimism and seized **20M OP** sent to Wintermute's address, because that Safe existed on mainnet but not on Optimism ([Rekt](https://rekt.news/wintermute-rekt)). "USDT" is also not one asset. Tron connects to USDT0 only through the Legacy Mesh ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15291261-best-cross-chain-usdt-bridge-2026-multi-network-steps)), and CCTP lists no Tron route ([Circle docs](https://developers.circle.com/cctp/required-block-confirmations)). So "any chain in, any chain out" is often a swap plus a bridge, not a transfer.

Finality makes it worse. CCTP Standard Transfer waits about 15–19 minutes on Ethereum L2s and **6–32 hours on Linea** ([Circle docs](https://developers.circle.com/cctp/required-block-confirmations)). That means instant point-of-sale credit requires someone to front capital and absorb reorg and fraud risk. Bridges are crypto's most exploited layer, with about **$2.9B lost across 34 incidents** from 2021 to Q3 2025 ([Global Ledger](https://app.globalledger.io/hubfs/Press%20Materials/All%20Bridge%20Hacks%2C%20%242.9B%20Lost.pdf)).

The legal barrier is decisive. FinCEN treats anyone who "accepts CVCs and retransmits them" as a money transmitter, while unhosted wallet providers generally are not ([FinCEN 2019](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf); [Katten](https://katten.com/fincen-publishes-guidance-pertaining-to-certain-business-models-involving-convertible-virtual-currencies)). The MiCA grandfathering period ended on **1 July 2026** ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-04/ESMA75-113276571-1679_Statement_on_the_end_of_transitional_periods_under_MiCA.pdf)). Under the EBA's position, a CASP that transfers e-money tokens such as USDC also needs PSD2 payment-institution authorisation ([EBA](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-publishes-no-action-letter-interplay-between-payment-services-directive-psd23-and-markets-crypto)). From about July 2028, US service providers may offer only permitted payment stablecoins ([Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/insights/2025/07/stablecoin-framework-signed-into-law)). A router that auto-swaps into bridged variants could therefore be making a regulated offer.

Issuer freezes add tail risk. In March 2026 Circle blacklisted a bridge contract, DFINITY's ckBridge, and users' withdrawals failed ([DFINITY forum](https://forum.dfinity.org/t/ckbridge-ethereum-address-blacklisted-by-circle-usdc-withdrawals-failing/65773)). The BIS argues that stablecoin fragmentation "cannot be easily solved with interoperability of different blockchains" ([BIS AER 2025](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2025e3.htm)).

### No shared name directory, because nobody accountable runs one

UPI and Pix directories work because a regulated operator admits only licensed institutions and carries liability. UPI TPAPs operate under tripartite agreements in which PSP banks hold audit rights and fraud liability ([NPCI Circular 32](https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/upi/circular/2017/UPI%20Circular%20no%2032_Multi-Bank%20Approach_15th%20Sept.pdf)). Even with institution-only access, Brazil's Pix directory leaked: one bank reported "undue consultations" of **395,009 keys** ([Baguete](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/vazam-395-mil-chaves-pix)). Crypto's privacy-preserving attempt, Celo's ODIS, blinds phone numbers before lookup ([Celo docs](https://docs.celo.org/celo-codebase/protocol/identity/phone-number-privacy)) but rations queries by quota ([Celo ODIS](https://docs.celo.org/developer/contractkit/odis)), and its repository appears archived **[UNVERIFIED]** ([mirror](https://gh.r-mj.com/celo-org/SocialConnect)). European data regulators warn that hashes of phone numbers are easy to re-identify ([Covington](https://www.insideprivacy.com/data-privacy/spanish-supervisory-authority-and-edps-release-guidance-on-hashing-for-data-pseudonymization-and-anonymization-purposes/)), which fits badly with permanent on-chain storage. ENS is the neutral namespace, but at about **2.0M names** **[secondary, undated]** ([dn.org](https://dn.org/measuring-the-web3-domain-market-what-2025-reports-reveal/)) it is tiny next to UPI. Nothing ties an ENS name to a verified person the way UPI shows the payee's bank-KYC'd name before payment. The research also found that handles are growth moats for Binance, Telegram, Venmo and World, though no source documents any of them refusing to interoperate.

### No refunds or disputes, because chargebacks are paid for by interchange

Card disputes are expensive and paid for by someone. Mastercard estimates **261M chargebacks** in 2025, worth **$33.79B**. Large merchants report $151.88 of internal cost per chargeback, and about 45% of chargeback volume is fraudulent ([Mastercard](https://b2b.mastercard.com/news-and-insights/blog/what-s-the-true-cost-of-a-chargeback-in-2025)). Stablecoin acceptance sells on low fees, so no margin exists to fund a dispute desk. The law does not force one either. The CFPB proposed applying Reg E to stablecoins in January 2025 ([Ballard Spahr](https://www.fintechanddigitalassets.com/2025/01/cfpb-proposes-extending-electronic-funds-transfer-act-protections-to-stablecoin-and-gaming-accounts/)), and the proposal was reportedly withdrawn **[UNVERIFIED]** ([risktemplate](https://risktemplate-site.pages.dev/blog/2026-04-17-reg-e-efta-crypto-compliance-stablecoin-genius-act)). Consumer Reports says the GENIUS Act provides no error-resolution procedure ([Consumer Reports](https://advocacy.consumerreports.org/press_release/house-passes-genius-act-that-fails-to-protect-consumers-in-stablecoin-market)).

The building blocks do exist. Coinbase and Shopify's Commerce Payments Protocol puts an audited escrow with authorize, capture, void, reclaim and refund between buyer and merchant ([Base blog](https://blog.base.dev/commerce-payments-protocol)). Kleros offers arbitration for low-value e-commerce disputes ([Kleros docs](https://docs.kleros.io/products/enterprise)). Users are asking for this. A 3 November 2026 post in r/StableCoins argued that "a refund needs its own transaction record" ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableCoins/comments/1xn5pf7/with_direct_stablecoin_payments_a_refund_needs/)), and a dispute-evidence proposal for x402 was still open in late October ([TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/43002)). Buyer protection invites friendly fraud, so it should be priced and arbitrated, not given away.

### No mandate standard, because the only universal pull primitive is the drainer's favourite tool

On EVM chains, `approve` and Permit signatures are both the only universal pull mechanism and the main theft vector. Signature phishing cost about **$494M in 2024** and **$83.85M in 2025**, and Permit/Permit2 accounted for 38% of large-case losses ([KuCoin summary of Scam Sniffer](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/2025-crypto-phishing-losses-drop-83-to-83-85m-drainer-ecosystem-remains-active)). The safe replacement, scoped ERC-7715 permissions, works only on smart accounts and in practice means MetaMask 13.23.0 or later. The method name has already changed from `wallet_grantPermissions` to `wallet_requestExecutionPermissions` ([MetaMask docs](https://docs.metamask.io/smart-accounts-kit/concepts/advanced-permissions/); [older docs](https://docs.metamask.io/delegation-toolkit/concepts/erc7715)). There is no equivalent of UPI AutoPay's NPCI-hosted mandate, with its standard pause and revoke screens in every app. On-chain, a bad pull is final. Even NPCI concluded that P2P pull was too dangerous ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/08/223-npci-p2p-collect-payments-oct-1-what-it-means/)).

### No offline payments, because double-spend prevention needs hardware nobody has built for stablecoins

The BIS Polaris handbook treats double-spending as a core risk of offline payments and relies on tamper-resistant devices to manage it ([BIS](https://www.bis.org/publications/project-polaris-handbook-offline-payments-cbdc)). Uruguay's offline e-peso pilot left double-spending and lost-wallet thresholds unresolved ([arXiv 2305.02290](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.02290)). Feature-phone access is a separate and solvable problem. Kotani Pay runs USSD stablecoin menus ([Kotani docs](https://docs.kotanipay.com/docs/kotani-pay-ussd)), but USSD is custodial and mobile operators set its price. Kenya's competition authority had to push Safaricom's USSD session charges down to KES 2 ([Business Daily Africa](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/corporate/CAK-forces-Safaricom-cut-cost-mobile-banking/539550-3852854-2nqj5y/index.html)). Kotani itself raised only a $2M pre-seed ([The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/crypto-web3-and-cbdc/news/kenyas-startup-kotani-pay-gets-usd-2-mln-funding)).

### No dual-rail QR, because national schemes own the QR tags

EMVCo's merchant-presented QR can carry several payment programmes in one code ([EMVCo](https://www.emvco.com/knowledge-hub/the-what-why-and-how-of-emv-qr-codes/)), but central banks own the national profiles, as Namibia's NAMQR shows ([Bank of Namibia](https://www.bon.com.na/getattachment/Bank/National-Payment-System/Legal-Framework/Standards/NAMQR-CODE-STANDARDS-V5-0.pdf.aspx?lang=en-ZA)). The first serious bridge is **X9.150**, published by ANSI's ASC X9 in July 2026. On 19 October 2026 it was demonstrated with one signed QR payable over FedNow, RTP or USDC on Solana. The participants stated plainly that it "is not a product launch" ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/x9150-qr-code-payments-demo-at-money2020-lets-one-invoice-be-paid-over-fednow-rtp-or-usdc-on-solana); [Business Wire](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-10-19-matera-payfinia-and-solana-foundation-demonstrate-new-us-qr-code-payment-standard-at-money2020)). Altitude noted that a valid signature "does not establish that the underlying invoice is legitimate" ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/x9150-qr-code-payments-demo-at-money2020-lets-one-invoice-be-paid-over-fednow-rtp-or-usdc-on-solana)). The practical route today is the exchange model: Bybit Pay pays Pix and AEON QR codes custodially, one country at a time ([Electronic Payments Intl](https://www.electronicpaymentsinternational.com/news/bybit-pay-vietnam-philippines/)).

### Weak merchant adoption, because consumers and merchants are each waiting for the other

In an FIS survey, **52.7%** of US consumers want at least half of merchants to accept digital currency before they will seriously use it ([FIS](https://www.fisglobal.com/about-us/media-room/press-release/2025/fis-research-banks-hold-the-key-to-stablecoin-adoption)). Meanwhile, 90% of merchants say they would accept it if setup were as simple as cards ([Chain Store Age](https://chainstoreage.com/survey-online-merchants-continue-accepting-crypto-payment-option)). Distribution alone has not solved this. The Coinbase/Shopify payments protocol reportedly processed only about **$1.2M USDC** in its first months **[UNVERIFIED]** ([Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/crypto/news/payments-protocol-by-coinbase-shopify-processes-just-12m-usdc-since-june-growthepie-61c89980-5b85-479e-bacd-2e933928812b/)). US tax treats each stablecoin spend as a property disposal, and the PARITY Act fix is unenacted ([Baker McKenzie](https://blockchain.bakermckenzie.com/2026/04/17/the-parity-acts-de-minimis-rewrite-and-the-bitcoin-coffee-problem-it-leaves-unsolved/)). In a vendor-sponsored survey, **62%** of US holders had converted back to dollars because merchants would not accept stablecoins ([Eastern Progress](https://www.easternprogress.com/mnee-pay-survey-reveals-millions-of-americans-hold-stablecoins-most-cant-spend-them/article_3d390148-d63c-5f75-8de4-d6c9c9c22fa8.html)). India adds its own frictions: a **6–7% USDT premium** over the reference rate in late October 2026 ([The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/10/21/india-stablecoin-premium-holds-near-records-usdt-usdc/)), 30% tax plus 1% TDS ([APAC Money](https://apacmoney.com/post/india-crypto-tax-regulation)), and RBI documents "leaning towards prohibition" ([The Coin Republic](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2026/07/06/crypto-regulation-news-rbi-pushes-banks-away-from-stablecoins/)).

## WondrPay v2 blueprint: be the rulebook and resolver, and let issuers move the money

*Everything in this section is design recommendation built on the evidence above. Facts carry citations; judgments do not.*

### Product thesis

WondrPay already has the hardest parts of the unbuilt layer: refunds, refund cases with Arkiv receipts, Autopay, ENS names, World ID and a merchant SDK. (These features come from the project brief; Arkiv was not covered in the research notes.) The v2 thesis is **"UPI's rulebook layer for stablecoins"**: a non-custodial app plus an open spec. The spec has four parts. First, a **handle resolver**: `name.eth` resolves to per-chain receive preferences and a verified display name. Second, a **signed payment-request object** that compiles to EIP-681 for EVM wallets and to Solana Pay for Solana wallets. Third, a **receipt, refund and dispute ledger** with published turnaround rules. Fourth, a **mandate registry** for Autopay with caps and one-tap revoke.

Money moves only through rails the issuer runs (CCTP, Gateway, USDT0), always signed by the user's own key. This is the design the evidence supports. It avoids the money-transmitter trigger of accepting and retransmitting value ([FinCEN 2019](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf)). It avoids the solver risk that cost Across ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/07/24/across-solana-relayer-attack/)). And it targets the gap no incumbent fills: buyer protection for final on-chain payments, which Stripe explicitly lacks ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/stablecoin-payments?locale=en-GB)). The user-facing promise is "pay any WondrPay name from wherever your dollars are, get a receipt, and get your money back if the merchant agrees or an arbiter rules". It is not "one address that magically works on every chain".

### AI features worth building, and the guardrails that make them credible

The evidence says AI in payments gains trust through limits, not autonomy. Revolut gates sensitive actions behind biometrics ([The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/revolut-launches-ai-assistant-air-in-ireland-and-europe)). Mastercard blocks agent purchases above the user's cap at the network ([ATXP](https://atxp.ai/blog/mastercard-agent-pay-stripe-acp-visa-x402-comparison-2026/)). And 89% of companies that do not use AI fraud tools cite fear of false positives ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cybersecurity/fraud-prevention/2026/what-fraud-data-says-about-where-payment-security-is-headed/)). Five features fit WondrPay's existing pieces.

The first is a **pre-sign payment explainer**. It simulates every transaction, flags drainer-style approvals and Permit requests (the main 2024–25 loss vector), checks the payee against the resolver's verified name, and explains any flag in plain language. The second is an **agent sub-wallet built on Autopay mandates**: per-merchant, per-day and per-transaction caps, a World ID step-up above the cap, and x402 support so the agent can pay APIs. This copies the network pattern and prevents the unlimited-signing failure in the x402 SDK flaw ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-x402-sdk-flaw-could-let.html)). The third is a **refund-case copilot**. It assembles evidence from Arkiv receipts, drafts the merchant response, and proposes a resolution within the published turnaround time. This targets the open x402 dispute-evidence gap ([TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/43002)). The fourth is a **merchant reconciliation agent** that matches incoming payments to invoices, exports cost-basis-neutral accounting records, and answers "who hasn't paid". The fifth is **natural-language pay** ("send Priya 20 USDT") that resolves names and always shows a confirm screen.

Keep agents read-only by default. Do not pitch x402 volume as traction: only 0.6–7.5% of x402 value is actually agentic ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/agentic-payments-are-growing-most-x402-payments-are-not-from-ai-agents)).

### Business model

UPI shows that zero fees with no payer collapses into a subsidy, and India is moving to a 0.4% MDR above ₹2,000 ([Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/upi-hits-record-25-47-bn-transactions-in-october-daily-value-crosses-inr-1-l-cr)). Price under the incumbents and charge for protection. P2P stays free and is paid for by the relayer budget. The merchant fee should sit below Stripe's and PayPal's 1.5% ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14895614-stablecoin-payment-processor-fees-compared-2026)) and compete with Whitepay's 0.4–1.5% ([whitepay.com](https://whitepay.com/)). Something like 0.5–0.8% including refunds and receipts is a judgment, not a sourced figure. On top of that, sell an **opt-in buyer-protection fee** that funds the loss reserve. The research estimates this at 0.3–1%; that is an unsourced inference. FX spread can come from Arc's App Kit, which lets developers set a spread fee ([Arc FX](https://docs.arc.io/build/stablecoin-fx.md)). Card interchange can come through a licensed issuer partner such as Rain, which is now valued at $1.95B on card issuing ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/384910/rain-valuation-nears-2-billion-usd-series-c-250-million-raise)). Finally, sell a **B2B resolver, refund and mandate SDK** to other wallets.

Avoid building the model on float yield. The OCC's GENIUS rule restates the yield ban ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/381502/occ-finalizes-genius-act-stablecoin-rules-ahead-of-january-deadline)), and advertised APYs in the market are already drawing scepticism: a tester measured 3.9% against an advertised "up to 6%" ([kkinvesting](https://kkinvesting.io/en/posts/plasma-one-card/)).

### Regulatory path (not legal advice)

Design so that **the user's key signs every leg and WondrPay never holds or retransmits value**. Under FinCEN's 2019 guidance that is the unhosted-wallet branch, which is generally not an MSB ([FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf)). Your relayer pays gas but should never take custody. Get a legal opinion on the relayer and the escrow contracts, because FinCEN's guidance has no time-based exemption for brief custody ([CryptoSlate profile](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-laws/fincen-2019-convertible-virtual-currency-guidance-usa/)). Run every fiat ramp and card through licensed partners. In the EU, a service that transfers USDC for clients may need both a CASP authorisation and a PSD2 licence ([EBA](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-publishes-no-action-letter-interplay-between-payment-services-directive-psd23-and-markets-crypto)), so partner rather than build. WhiteBIT's group reportedly obtained MiCA authorisation in Austria **[UNVERIFIED]** ([yespress](https://yespress.io/whitebit.md)), which could make it a natural partner.

In the US, GENIUS takes effect on **18 January 2027** ([OCC](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-92.html)), and by about July 2028 providers may offer only permitted stablecoins ([Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/insights/2025/07/stablecoin-framework-signed-into-law)). Keep a per-jurisdiction token allowlist. Singapore criminalises unlicensed offshore-only service ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/sgp/legal-updates/singapore-s-june-30-hard-deadline-for-crypto-firms-serving-overseas-customers)). Dubai requires a licence per activity ([Farahat & Co](https://farahatco.com/blog/vara-virtual-assets-and-related-activities-regulations)). In **India**, serving users requires FIU-IND registration, and FIU-IND blocked 25 offshore platforms in October 2025 ([Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/10/223-fiu-ind-notices-25-offshore-crypto-platforms-pmla-violation/)). Treat India as an NRI-remittance story through licensed partners, not domestic merchant payments. Block sanctioned addresses at both ends, and budget for issuer freezes that can strand in-flight funds ([AMLBot](https://blog.amlbot.com/circle-froze-16-business-hot-wallets-including-a-blockchain-bridge-smart-contract/)).

The pitch should not be "another wallet". Pure consumer wallets without licensed ramps or volume are not where the large cheques went. Pitch "**the buyer-protection and mandate layer stablecoin payments are missing**": refunds, receipts and capped mandates as an open spec, with Whitechain merchant volume as proof. That frames WondrPay as the kind of clean, licensable rail incumbents have been buying.

### What to avoid

**Do not run your own bridge or solver.** Bridges lost about $2.9B in 34 incidents ([Global Ledger](https://app.globalledger.io/hubfs/Press%20Materials/All%20Bridge%20Hacks%2C%20%242.9B%20Lost.pdf)), and even sound contracts failed through Across's off-chain reader ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/07/24/across-solana-relayer-attack/)). **Do not promise "send to my address on any chain"** for smart accounts unless the account is deployed there first. The Wintermute loss shows why ([Rekt](https://rekt.news/wintermute-rekt)). **Do not ship P2P collect or unlimited approvals.** NPCI banned the first ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/npci-to-end-upi-p2p-collect-requests-from-october-1-to-reduce-fraud)), and the second fuels drainers ([KuCoin/Scam Sniffer](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/2025-crypto-phishing-losses-drop-83-to-83-85m-drainer-ecosystem-remains-active)). **Do not go custodial-card-first.** Custody freezes are the top complaint about KAST ([Medium](https://medium.com/@gemQueenx/kast-card-review-2026-best-crypto-cards-alternatives-46ff6792f939)), and RedotPay is in litigation ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/private-equity/binance-claims-redotpay-diverted-users-in-470-million-lawsuit)). **Do not build for Indian domestic retail** while the RBI leans toward prohibition ([The Coin Republic](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2026/07/06/crypto-regulation-news-rbi-pushes-banks-away-from-stablecoins/)). **Do not depend on one vendor's abstraction layer.** OneBalance shut its API ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/blog/migrating-from-onebalance-to-zerion-api/)), and Coinbase moved and renamed the Base Account SDK ([docs.base.org](https://docs.base.org/base-account/guides/accept-payments)). **Do not use the name Whitepay** anywhere user-facing, because W Group runs a live product by that name ([whitepay.com](https://whitepay.com/)).

## Conclusion

Most of the research points the same way. The stablecoin payment race is settling its infrastructure layer through consolidation: Stripe owns Bridge and Tempo, Circle owns Arc and CPN, Mastercard owns BVNK, and settlement speed is no longer scarce. What stays scarce is the institutional layer that let UPI scale: who is liable when a payment goes wrong, what a mandate may pull, and how a name is verified. Those gaps persist because filling them costs money and creates liability, not because they are hard to code. That favours a startup that turns them into an open spec plus a paid protection product, and it disfavours one that tries to out-route the issuers. Whitechain's lack of payments primitives is useful here: it leaves WondrPay's relayer, refunds and receipts as the only payment layer in a 10M-user ecosystem.

The biggest uncertainties are in the plan's foundations, so check them before committing. Official USDT on Whitechain mainnet has no confirmed date. ERC-7828 and ERC-7715 may change before they are finalised. The legal status of a gas relayer and refund escrow under money-transmission law has never been ruled on. And the user-sentiment evidence behind "people want refunds" is thin, because X was unavailable and Reddit was blocked during the research. The most useful next experiment is cheap. Put refund-protected checkout in front of 20 WhiteBIT-corridor merchants and measure whether buyers pay more, return more often, or both. That single number tells you whether the protection fee, the core of the business model, is real.
